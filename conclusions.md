Key Findings & Conclusions

1. Country Performance
The analysis covered 11 countries and identified significant differences in account distribution and email campaign activity.
- The United States ranked first in both total accounts (12,384) and sent messages (233,503).
- India ranked second, with 2,687 accounts and 51,189 sent messages.
- Canada ranked third, with 2,067 accounts and 37,914 sent messages.
- The United States, India, and Canada accounted for approximately 80% of all sent messages.
China demonstrated a difference between its rankings: it ranked 8th in total accounts but 5th in sent messages. This suggests a relatively higher email activity compared with its account base.

2. Email Campaign Performance
Across the analyzed period, the dataset contained:
- 403,014 sent messages.
- 143,103 email opens.
- 15,754 link visits.
The aggregate ratios were:
- Opens per sent message: 35.51%.
- Visits per sent message: 3.91%.
These figures represent ratios of aggregated events and should not necessarily be interpreted as unique-user Open Rate or Click-Through Rate.
December 2020 recorded the highest number of sent messages (151,496), followed by January 2021 (138,795).

3. User Segmentation
The account distribution by verification status showed:
- Verified accounts: 15,266 (71.58%).
- Unverified accounts: 6,061 (28.42%).
Regarding subscription status:
- Accounts without an unsubscribe flag: 17,684 (82.92%).
- Accounts with an unsubscribe flag: 3,643 (17.08%).
The one-day sending interval represented the largest segment, accounting for 21,074 accounts.
Other sending intervals had considerably smaller account volumes, so their engagement ratios should be interpreted cautiously.

4. Key Insights
1. Email campaign activity was concentrated in three countries: the United States, India, and Canada.
2. The United States was the largest market by both account volume and email activity.
3. The distribution of email activity was not identical to the distribution of accounts across countries.
4. Most accounts had verified status.
5. The one-day sending interval dominated the dataset.
6. December 2020 was the most active month in terms of sent messages.

5. Recommendations
1. Continue monitoring email campaign performance in the United States, India, and Canada, as these countries represent the largest share of activity.
2. Investigate differences in email engagement between countries to identify opportunities for campaign optimization.
3. Evaluate email performance across verification and subscription segments while accounting for differences in audience size.
4. Conduct further analysis of sending intervals using comparable audience groups and sufficient sample sizes.
5. Monitor monthly campaign activity to identify changes in email engagement over time.

6. Data Limitations
- The dataset covers the period from November 1, 2020, to February 25, 2021.
- February represents an incomplete month.
- Aggregated email events do not necessarily represent unique users.
- Differences in segment sizes should be considered when comparing engagement metrics.
- Further investigation is required before making causal conclusions about the effectiveness of specific sending intervals or user segments.
